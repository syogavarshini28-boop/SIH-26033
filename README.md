## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

1. Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
We propose developing an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), retailers, wholesalers, hotels and consumers without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform will provide the following features:
- Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Marketplace: Farmers can list their agricultural products, quantities, quality and prices directly on the platform.
- AI-Based Price Recommendation: AI analyzes market demand, supply and historical prices to suggest fair selling prices.
- Bulk Buying System: Retailers, wholesalers and businesses can purchase agricultural products directly from farmers in bulk.
- Smart Logistics Management: The platform connects farmers with transport providers to arrange affordable delivery.
- Demand Forecasting: AI predicts future demand for agricultural products to help farmers plan their production and sales.
- Product Quality Verification: Buyers can view product details, harvest dates, quality grades and available certifications.
- Multilingual Interface: The application supports regional languages to make it accessible to rural farmers.
- Secure Digital Payments: Integrated digital payment facilities provide transparent and reliable transactions.
2. How it addresses the problem
- Eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by connecting farmers directly with buyers.
- Reduces additional commissions and transportation costs.
- Provides farmers with better price information and direct market access.
- Helps consumers purchase fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices.
- Reduces food wastage through demand prediction and efficient distribution.
- Improves transparency in agricultural transactions.
3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution
- AI-powered dynamic pricing based on market conditions.
- Intelligent matching of farmers with nearby buyers.
- AI-based demand forecasting to minimize overproduction and shortages.
- Route optimization to reduce transportation time and expenses.
- A combined marketplace and logistics platform.
- A farmer-friendly interface with regional language and voice-assisted features.
- Digital transaction records to improve trust and transparency.

## Feasibility and Viability

1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
The proposed digital agricultural marketplace is technically, economically and operationally feasible because it uses existing digital technologies and infrastructure.
- Technical Feasibility: Web and mobile technologies, cloud platforms, AI and machine learning can be used to develop the application.
- Economic Feasibility: The platform can operate with low initial infrastructure costs by using cloud services and existing transportation networks.
- Operational Feasibility: Farmers, FPOs, retailers and consumers can access the platform through smartphones.
- Market Feasibility: There is a strong need for transparent agricultural pricing and direct connections between producers and buyers.
- Scalability: The platform can initially be implemented in selected villages and gradually expanded across districts and states.
2. Potential challenges and risks
- Limited internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Lack of digital literacy among some farmers.
- Difficulty maintaining consistent product quality.
- Transportation delays and delivery management issues.
- Price fluctuations due to changing market demand.
- Data security and payment-related risks.
- Difficulty building trust between farmers and new buyers.
3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges
- Provide a simple, multilingual and voice-assisted application.
- Introduce offline data entry and SMS notifications.
- Conduct digital awareness and training programmes for farmers.
- Implement product grading and quality verification.
- Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.
- Use AI to monitor market trends and provide updated price recommendations.
- Implement secure authentication, encrypted data storage and verified digital payments.
- Provide customer support and a complaint resolution system.
